Introduction

Patient-first dentistry in Pala

Smile Architects is a multispeciality dental clinic and advanced orthodontic centre on Kattakkayam Road, Pala, Kottayam, Kerala — near Federal Bank in Pala Town.

The clinic provides comprehensive dental care for patients in Pala and the surrounding Meenachil region, serves Kottayam District, and operates a Thrissur branch in Punkunnam. General dentistry is combined with orthodontics and cosmetic care under one roof.

Led by Dr. Jeo Tom Charls, MDS Orthodontics — registered with the Kerala State Dental Council — the clinical team places patient comfort and accurate diagnosis at the centre of every consultation.

Preventive care, check-ups and routine dental treatment — Smile Architects, Pala.

Clinical leadership

Your orthodontic specialist

Dr. Jeo Tom Charls
MDS — Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics

Dr. Jeo Tom Charls is the principal orthodontist at Smile Architects, Pala. An MDS-qualified specialist in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics, he is a Certified Invisalign and Spark Aligner Provider with advanced training in lingual orthodontics and CAD/CAM bracket systems.
