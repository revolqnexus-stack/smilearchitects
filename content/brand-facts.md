## 14. BRAND VOICE GUIDELINES

### Tone Characteristics
- **Professional**: Medical credibility, qualified expertise
- **Approachable**: Warm, patient-centered, reassuring
- **Specific**: Use concrete details, not vague claims
- **Honest**: Realistic expectations, transparent about limitations
- **Educational**: Help patients understand treatments

### Writing Style
- Use "we" for clinic team
- Use "you" for patients
- Use doctor names when discussing specific expertise
- Explain clinical terms when first mentioned
- Short paragraphs for readability
- Active voice preferred

### Credential Presentation Standards
✅ **Correct**: "Dr. Jeo Tom Charls, MDS Orthodontics"  
✅ **Correct**: "Dr. Jeo Tom Charls (MDS — Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics)"  
❌ **Avoid**: "Dr. Jeo" without qualification on first mention  
❌ **Avoid**: "Our orthodontist" without naming doctor

### Treatment Description Format
1. What it is (clinical definition)
2. Why it's needed (conditions addressed)
3. How it works (process overview)
4. What to expect (patient experience)
5. Who provides it (specialist attribution)
