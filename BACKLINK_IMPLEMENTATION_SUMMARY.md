## 🎓 KEY CONCEPTS

### Referring Domain vs. Backlink

- **Backlink:** A single link from another website to yours
- **Referring Domain:** A unique website linking to you (can have multiple backlinks)
- **What matters more:** Referring domains (20 domains linking once each > 1 domain linking 20 times)

### Link Attributes

- **Dofollow:** Passes SEO value (most valuable, but not required)
- **Nofollow:** Tells Google not to count for rankings (still valuable for traffic and brand)
- **Sponsored:** Used for paid partnerships (must be used to comply with Google guidelines)
- **UGC:** User-generated content links
- **Don't obsess:** Nofollow links from authoritative, relevant sites still help

### Link Quality Signals

✅ **High-Quality Link:**
- From Kerala/healthcare/local business website
- Editorial placement (earned, not paid)
- Contextual (in relevant content)
- From site with real traffic
- From site trusted by Google

❌ **Low-Quality Link:**
- From irrelevant foreign directory
- Sitewide footer link
- From spammy blog network
- From site with no real content
- From site penalized by Google

## 📞 SUPPORT & RESOURCES

### If You Get Stuck:

**Problem: No responses to outreach**
- Solution: Make the story more newsworthy first (conduct camp, then pitch coverage)
- Review email templates - are you offering value or asking for favors?
- Try different contact methods (phone vs email)

**Problem: Don't have time for this**
- Solution: Delegate to marketing staff member (provide them this documentation)
- Minimum viable effort: 2-3 hours/week can still generate 10-15 quality links in 90 days
- Focus on highest-impact tactics only (directories + one school camp)

**Problem: School camps feel overwhelming**
- Solution: Start with one small school (50-100 students)
- Keep it simple: 30-minute education session + basic screening
- Partner with service organization to share responsibility
- Use camp as PR opportunity - makes the effort worthwhile

**Problem: Media won't cover us**
- Solution: You need a story worth covering first
  - "Local dentist offers free camp" = maybe newsworthy
  - "200 schoolchildren receive first-ever dental screening thanks to local clinic partnership" = definitely newsworthy
- Lead with the community benefit, not the clinic promotion

**Problem: Links not appearing in GSC**
- Solution: Wait 2-4 weeks for Google to discover and index
- Submit linked pages to GSC manually if needed
- Verify link is actually live (check source code)
- Some links take months to appear - be patient

### Measuring Success:

**Month 1:** 5-8 new referring domains (mostly directories - quick wins)
**Month 2:** 3-5 new domains (educational partnerships starting to pay off)
**Month 3:** 5-8 new domains (media coverage from camps, ongoing partnerships)

**Total after 90 days:** 15-20 new referring domains if following plan consistently

**If you have fewer:** Review what you actually executed. Did you send outreach emails? Conduct camps? Most "failures" are execution failures, not strategy failures.

**If you have more:** Excellent! Double down on what worked. Scale successful tactics.

**Common questions answered:**

**Q: Can I hire someone to do this?**
A: Yes, but supervise closely. Many SEO agencies will take shortcuts. Use this documentation to train and monitor them. Insist on white-hat tactics only.

**Q: Should I use Ahrefs/SEMrush?**
A: Not necessary for local link building. Google Search Console is free and sufficient. Premium tools are nice-to-have, not must-have.

**Q: How much time does this actually take?**
A: Week 1-2: 5-7 hours (setup). Weeks 3-12: 3-5 hours/week. Camp days: 1 full day (quarterly). Ongoing: 2-3 hours/week.

**Q: Can we just focus on Google Ads instead?**
A: Ads and SEO serve different purposes. Ads = immediate leads but ongoing cost. SEO = long-term authority and free organic traffic. Do both if budget allows, but SEO compounds over time.

**Q: What if competitors are buying links and ranking?**
A: They're playing Russian roulette with Google penalties. Build sustainable authority instead. When (not if) Google catches them, you'll still be ranking.
